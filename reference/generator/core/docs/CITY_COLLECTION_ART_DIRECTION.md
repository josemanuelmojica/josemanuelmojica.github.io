# Street Choreographies / Volume 01

## Subject and job

This is an eight-piece collection of real American street networks prepared as text-free animation assets for editorial and portfolio websites. Its job is to let the street geometry—not a city title—carry the identity of each place.

## Collection rules

- No visible city, state, country, coordinate, or attribution typography inside the artwork.
- OpenStreetMap attribution remains in the surrounding product or page chrome.
- The full road network remains static and legible; 3,000 spatially distributed roads form the candidate pool.
- A typical sequence activates 120 pieces, gathers them into one formation, then restores the exact source geometry.
- Water, parks, and negative space remain anchoring fields rather than moving particles.
- Each study uses one dominant formation and propagation pairing, but the assets retain the shared motion API.

## Composition

The map occupies the full frame with no conventional poster footer. Crops favor the structural feature that distinguishes the network: coastline, river, lakefront, bridge crossings, orthogonal grid, or radial beltway. The road network is the hero and animation is the only reveal.

The collection intentionally avoids a generic single-color dark mode. Its cohesion comes from line hierarchy, negative-space discipline, identical motion timing, and the absence of labels—not from forcing every city into one palette.
