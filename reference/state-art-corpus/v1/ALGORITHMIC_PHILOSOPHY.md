# Quiet Watersheds

Quiet Watersheds treats every state as a field of paper under measurement: a recognizable boundary held lightly inside a larger drafting surface. The computational composition begins with real first-order geography, fits it without changing its topology, and leaves enough white margin for the shape to breathe. Beauty comes from the repeatable transformation—source geometry, local projection, measured fit, and consistent framing—not from a one-off illustration.

Water is the moving intelligence of the system. Real river and lake-centerline geometry is clipped to each state and rendered as a hierarchy of blueprint-blue washes and ink strokes. Scale rank controls weight, so continental rivers pull the eye while smaller recorded channels sit closer to pencil pressure. The meticulously crafted algorithm never invents a watercourse to fill an empty composition; restraint and geographic honesty are part of the aesthetic.

Graph paper supplies a second, quieter coordinate system. Minor and major rules, datum circles, registration crosses, and barely visible paper fibers are generated from a stable seed derived from the ISO state code. The same state therefore reproduces exactly, while small state-to-state changes keep the corpus from feeling stamped out. This controlled variation is the product of deep computational care: every mark is subordinate to the silhouette and every parameter is tuned for a coherent fifty-image family.

The projection is intentionally local and editorial. Longitude is corrected by the state’s mean latitude, antimeridian geometry is unwrapped for Alaska, and every silhouette is fitted to the same four-by-three plate without claiming survey precision. A master-level implementation should make these mathematical decisions disappear into the experience; difficult shapes such as Alaska, Hawaii, Maryland, and Rhode Island must feel composed rather than treated as exceptions.

The final movement lives in reproducibility. One pinned public-domain dataset, one versioned generator, and one deterministic configuration produce both scalable masters and compact runtime images, complete with hashes and a contact sheet. Countless visual iterations should remain possible by changing explicit parameters, but any approved version must be painstakingly regenerated, reviewed as a whole, and traceable back to its exact geometry and code.
